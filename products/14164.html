Three-Strand Tahitian and Akoya Necklace

Three strands — layering done in one piece

Three 18K gold strands place grey-toned Akoya and platinum-grey pearls at different heights, creating a layered composition in one piece. Smaller Akoya pearls and larger grey pearls are spaced to give each element room to stand out.
