The pearl guide

Golden, white South Sea, Tahitian, Akoya and freshwater pearls: where each comes from, how it looks, and the pieces we make with it.

Golden pearls

Golden pearls are grown in the gold-lipped variety of the large Pinctada maxima oyster, the same oyster that gives white South Sea pearls. Their colour runs from pale champagne and cream to deep gold.

  • In our pieces: cream-gold to deep gold
  • Sizes in our pieces: 8–15 mm
  • Set in 18K gold, 14K gold, sterling silver or copper alloy, depending on the piece

White South Sea pearls

White South Sea pearls come from the silver-lipped Pinctada maxima oyster. They are among the largest cultured pearls, with a silvery-white colour and a soft, satin glow.

  • In our pieces: white, including drop-shaped pearls
  • Sizes in our pieces: 8–13 mm
  • Shown on their own, or mixed with golden and Tahitian pearls

Tahitian pearls

Tahitian pearls grow in the black-lipped Pinctada margaritifera oyster. Their natural colours run from silvery and platinum grey to near-black, often with green, blue or rosé overtones.

  • In our pieces: platinum grey and darker greys, including baroque shapes
  • Sizes in our pieces: 8–14 mm
  • Easy to wear with black, white, grey and denim

Akoya pearls

Akoya pearls come from the small Pinctada fucata oyster. They are smaller than South Sea and Tahitian pearls, usually round, with a bright, mirror-like lustre.

  • In our pieces: white and grey-toned Akoya
  • Sizes in our pieces: 3–8.5 mm
  • Used as accents between larger pearls, or three in a row on a ring

Freshwater pearls

Freshwater pearls grow in mussels in lakes and ponds, including those around Zhuji where XIAI is based. They come in the widest range of shapes, sizes and colours.

  • In our pieces: white and purple
  • Sizes in our pieces: 3–10 mm
  • We also offer premium freshwater, Edison and freshwater baroque pearls. Ask us what is in stock

Mabe & baroque pearls

Mabe pearls are cultured against the inside of the shell, which gives them a domed shape and a flat back. Baroque pearls grow in irregular shapes, so every one is different.

Our baroque piece is the Baroque Tahitian Pearl Necklace. For Mabe pieces such as the cocktail ring shown here, ask us what is currently available.
