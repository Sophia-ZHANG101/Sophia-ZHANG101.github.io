Our story

Rooted in Shanxiahu, at the source of China's pearl industry

XIAI Jewelry is based in Shanxiahu Town, Zhuji — the heart of China's pearl industry. Drawing on the region's pearl resources, we select pearls, develop and design products, craft them and sell them.

Our showroom, online store and personal advisory service connect with every kind of buyer, while dedicated teams for product selection, crafting, quality control, packing, shipping and after-sales work together behind each order. Beyond retail customers, we also supply sales channels and partner with livestream creators and on custom projects.

From the source

Close to every step of a pearl's journey

Working within the Shanxiahu pearl belt keeps us close to farming, harvesting, grading and raw pearl supply.

01

Farming

Pearls grow in mussels on the lakes and ponds around Zhuji.

02

Harvesting

Mussels are opened by hand and the pearls collected.

03

Measuring

Each pearl is sized so strands and pairs match.

04

Grading

Pearls are sorted by colour, shape and quality and kept in graded stock.

FAQ

Questions we are often asked

What does XIAI sell?

Pearl necklaces, earrings, rings, bracelets, pendants and brooches.

Do you only sell freshwater pearls?

No. Our focus is saltwater pearls — South Sea gold, Australian white, Tahitian, Akoya and Mabe — and we also offer premium freshwater, Edison and baroque pearls.

Are all settings 18K gold?

It depends on the piece. The metal is stated in each product's description.

What should I look at when choosing pearl jewelry?

The style, the pearl type, surface quality, size, the setting metal, real photos and the price. We are happy to send extra photos or videos of any piece.

Can you make custom pieces or supply wholesale?

Yes. Tell us what you need; costs and delivery are confirmed order by order.

How do certificates and after-sales service work?

Before you buy, we confirm which certificate comes with the specific piece and which after-sales terms apply.
