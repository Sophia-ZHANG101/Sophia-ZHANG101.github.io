Home / Our story

A pearl family since 1976

XIAI is based in Shanxiahu Town, Zhuji, the heart of China's pearl industry.

Drawing on the region's pearl resources, we select pearls, develop and design products, craft them and sell them.

Our showroom, online store and personal advisory service connect with every kind of buyer, while dedicated teams for product selection, crafting, quality control, packing, shipping and after-sales work together behind each order.

Beyond retail customers, we also supply sales channels and partner with livestream creators and on custom projects.

Milestones

Two generations, from the first pearls brought to market to today's showroom and partnerships.

  1. 1976The family begins working in the pearl industry.
  2. 1979First pearls brought to market.
  3. 1999The second generation takes over the business.
  4. 2008Moves into the East China International Jewelry City.
  5. 2010Registers the XIAI brand.
  6. 2012Starts selling through e-commerce.
  7. 2020Shifts toward livestream e-commerce.
  8. 2021–22Expands into creator partnerships, Douyin and WeChat Channels.
  9. 2023–nowBuilds out supply-chain and partnership services.

Close to every step of a pearl's journey

Working within the Shanxiahu pearl belt keeps us close to farming, harvesting, grading and raw pearl supply.

  1. Farming

    Pearls grow in mussels on the lakes and ponds around Zhuji.

  2. Harvesting

    Mussels are opened by hand and the pearls collected.

  3. Measuring

    Each pearl is sized so strands and pairs match.

  4. Grading

    Pearls are sorted by colour, shape and quality and kept in graded stock.

From brief to finished jewellery

Every new design follows the same five steps, so requirements are met and progress stays clear.

  1. Brief

    Agree the style, quantity and product requirements.

  2. Design

    Shape a first concept around the character of the pearls.

  3. Pearl matching

    Select pearls by size, colour and quality.

  4. Sampling & making

    Drilling, setting, assembly and the first sample.

  5. Final check

    Inspect structure, how it wears and overall quality.
